# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze employee workforce data, attrition, salary, job roles, satisfaction, age, gender, experience, and department-wise employee distribution.

## 🚀 Project Overview

Employee attrition is an important challenge for organizations because high employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect business performance.

This project uses Power BI to transform HR data into an interactive dashboard that helps HR teams and business stakeholders understand workforce patterns and identify factors associated with employee attrition.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Attrition by Department

Analyzes employee attrition across different departments:

- Administration
- Sales
- Operations
- IT
- Marketing
- Human Resources
- Finance

This helps identify departments with higher employee turnover.

### 2. Attrition by Salary Slab

Compares attrition across different salary ranges:

- 0–3 LPA
- 3–6 LPA
- 6–10 LPA
- 10+ LPA

This helps analyze the relationship between salary levels and employee attrition.

### 3. Attrition by Job Role & Job Satisfaction

Analyzes employee attrition based on:

- Job Role
- Job Satisfaction
- Total Attrition

This helps identify job roles and satisfaction levels associated with higher employee turnover.

### 4. Age Group Distribution

Shows employee distribution across different age groups:

- 18–25
- 26–35
- 36–45
- 46–55
- 55+

### 5. Attrition by Gender

Analyzes employee attrition based on gender:

- Male
- Female

### 6. Attrition Trend by Experience

Shows employee attrition based on total years of work experience.

This helps identify experience levels where employee turnover is more common.

### 7. Department-Wise Employee Count

Displays the total number of employees in each department and provides a quick overview of workforce distribution.

The dashboard includes interactive **Age Group** filters.

Selecting an age group dynamically updates the dashboard visuals.
